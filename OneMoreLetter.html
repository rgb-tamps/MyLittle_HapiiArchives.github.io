A letter

It's been a while since nakapag-usap tayo nang maayos. I know na baka ayaw mo nang makarinig ng words from me, or anything na may kinalaman sa atin. I know I've already taken so much of your peace, and the last thing I wanna do is disturb you again.

I want you to know na I don't expect a reply from you, and you don't have to respond to any of this. I'm not writing this to ask for anything, nor to make you feel obligated to say something back. I simply wanna let these words out, one last time, because there are things I've kept inside for so long, and I feel like I owe you the honesty I should've given you before.

For the last time, I wanna be selfish enough to reach out. Not because I expect you to come back, not because I want to change your mind, but because there's so much left unsaid inside me, and I don't think I've ever truly expressed how I feel about everything that happened between us.

It bothers me so much, knowing na ang dami nating unsaid thoughts, unresolved problems, and conversations we never got to finish. Ang dami nating bagay na hindi na naipaliwanag sa isa't isa, and the closure we never had still weighs heavily on me. I don't know if this letter will ever give us the closure we needed, but I hope, at the very least, it gives you a glimpse of what I've been carrying all this time.

I wanna make it clear to you na wala akong iba. I'm still holding on sa mga promises na binitawan ko sa'yo, even if I no longer know if those words still mean anything to you. I'm ashamed of myself, especially sa mga bagay na ginawa ko sa'yo. I'm sorry for not being strong enough to face you, our problems, and all the fights we had. I'm sorry for all the promises I failed to keep. If there's anything I promised you that I wasn't able to fulfill, I'm truly sorry.

I'm also sorry sa mga times na in-unfollow kita, then in-add, then in-unfollow ulit. I was so conflicted with everything that happened between us. I was hurt, angry, disappointed, and confused. Hindi ko na alam kung paano ko haharapin yung sarili kong feelings. I got so blinded by the things that hurt me, by the unfairness I felt, na hindi ko na nakita yung mga bagay na ginagawa ko rin sa'yo. I was so focused on the pain you caused me that I failed to recognize the pain I was causing you.

And the truth is, wala naman talaga akong iba. I just didn't know how to accept you again after everything that happened. Galit ako sa nangyari, pero mahal pa rin kita. I was caught between wanting to hold you close and wanting to protect myself from getting hurt again. I didn't know how to love you while carrying all that resentment, and I didn't know how to let you go while my heart was still choosing you.

I hate what happened to us. I hate the damage, the changes, and the distance that slowly grew between us. But what hurts me the most is seeing how much it changed you, and knowing that I was also part of the reason why.

Honestly, I don't think I can love someone else the same way again. Not being OA, but I know how much of a lover boy I am. I know the kind of love I'm capable of giving, the things I'm willing to sacrifice, and the lengths I would go to for someone I genuinely love. But even when I try to picture myself with someone else, I can't. I don't see myself building a future with another woman, knowing na hindi siya yung babaeng gusto kong pakasalan, makasama sa pagtanda, at maging nanay ng mga magiging anak ko someday.

I know it sounds selfish, and maybe it is. But even after everything, a part of me still sees you in the future I once dreamed of. I know I have no right to hold you back from the life you deserve. So maybe for now, I'll let myself yearn for you without anger. I'll let myself miss you without turning my pain into resentment. I'll let myself love you from a distance, even if it means watching you become everything you wanted to be without me.

You're still someone I wanna see shine. I wanna see you achieve your dreams, build the relationships you deserve, and become even happier than you were before. I wanna see you glow brighter, even in places where I no longer have a place beside you.

Andami ko ring natutunan lately. I'm genuinely thankful sa mga ate at kuya ko. Noong mga panahong wala akong makausap, wala akong mapaghingian ng advice, I always made decisions on my own. Ako lang lagi yung nag-a-advice sa sarili ko, without considering other perspectives. Kaya madalas, palpak yung mga decisions ko. I got used to being the kuya, the one who had to figure everything out on his own, the one who had to find answers without asking for help.

But recently, nagulat ako when my ate and kuya started asking me questions about what happened. They gave me different perspectives, different advice, and things I never thought about before. I cried because I realized how much I still didn't know about myself. Ang dami ko pa palang hindi nakikita, ang dami ko pa palang hindi naiintindihan, and for once, I felt like ako naman yung bunso. I felt like I had people who were willing to guide me, people who were willing to help me understand myself.

And you were right all along. I was toxic in so many ways. I finally started seeing my patterns. I'm always running away from situations I don't know how to handle because I hate the feelings they bring out in me. Instead of facing the problems, I choose to escape. Instead of communicating, I shut down. Instead of understanding, I let my emotions take over. And in doing so, I end up hurting the people who love me the most.

Looking back, I was naive. I know I'm still far from the person I wanna become, but I know I'll get there eventually. Tama ka rin pala, pain teaches a boy to be a man. And maybe this pain is teaching me the things I refused to learn when I still had you.

I don't wanna keep carrying this anger and resentment inside me just because you left and started outgrowing me. I saw you growing your relationships with the people around you, building connections, and becoming someone who had a life beyond us. And I hated how I felt less than you. I hated seeing you become better in ways I failed to become. I hated how your friends seemed to hold a higher place in your priorities than I did, and how I often felt like I came second to the people around you. But what I hated the most was realizing how much I envied you, not because I wanted you to have less, but because I wished I had the same growth, connections, and sense of belonging that you had.

I wish I had grown with you. I wish I had been someone you could be proud of. I wish I had become the person you needed while we were still together, instead of realizing all these things when it was already too late.

I know this is far from fixing us. I know your friends probably judge me and hate me because of the things I've done, and I understand them. They saw you hurt. They saw the things I failed to do. And if they wanna protect you from someone like me, I can't blame them.

I want you to protect your relationships with them. I want you to cherish the people who genuinely care about you. Do the things I failed to do. Give them the effort, comfort, and love I wasn't able to give you. And if seeing you surrounded by people who make you happy is all I get from now on, then I'll be happy for you, even if there's a part of me that hurts knowing I can no longer be one of those people.

Kahit wala nang sense yung sorry ko sa'yo, I still wanna say it. I'm genuinely sorry for everything I put you through. I'm sorry for the love you kept asking for na hindi ko naibigay. I'm sorry for the comfort, warmth, reassurance, and understanding you never got from me. I'm sorry for making you feel like you had to beg for things that should've come naturally from someone who claimed to love you.

I'm sorry for always realizing things too late. I'm sorry for always being late. I'm sorry for seeing the unfair things done to me more than the things I was doing to you. I'm sorry for not meeting you halfway. I'm sorry for making you feel unheard when all you wanted was to be understood. I'm sorry for making you feel alone when I was supposed to be your safe place.

I wish I had listened more. I wish I had understood your silence, your frustrations, and the things you were trying to tell me even when you were tired of explaining. I wish I had loved you in the way you needed, not only in the way I knew how.

I'm aware na a lot of the pain I'm feeling now is also something I brought upon myself. I had chances to do better. I had chances to be better. I had chances to save what we had, but I failed to recognize how important those chances were until they were gone.

I'm sorry for bleeding again. Siguro ito yung isa sa mga promises na hindi ko natupad sa'yo. I don't want to use my pain as a punishment for the things I've done. I wanna heal from the things that broke me, and I wanna become someone who no longer has to hurt himself just to feel or understand his own pain. I want to learn how to take care of myself, even when everything inside me feels heavy.

I know I still have a long way to go. I know I have so much to fix within myself. But I don't wanna remain the same person who lost you. I wanna grow from everything that happened, and I wanna become someone who knows how to love without hurting, someone who knows how to stay and face the problems instead of running away.

And despite everything, I still wanna thank you.

Thank you for being part of my life. Thank you for loving me in ways I didn't know how to appreciate back then. Thank you for the patience, the effort, the memories, and all the moments when you chose to stay even when I made it difficult for you.

Thank you for letting me experience a kind of love I once thought I'd never have. Thank you for being the woman I once imagined spending the rest of my life with. Thank you for the little things, the conversations, the laughter, the comfort, and the moments that probably seemed ordinary back then but now mean so much to me.

I'm sorry it took losing you for me to finally understand the things I should've understood while I still had you.

I hope someday, when you look back at us, hindi puro pain and disappointment yung maalala mo. I hope there were moments when you genuinely felt loved, happy, and appreciated. I hope the good memories still mean something to you, even if the ending wasn't what we wanted.

Sana ma-consider mo pa rin 'to as an effort from me, kahit hindi na ako yung taong para sa'yo. I'm really sorry it took me so long to renovate and do maintenance sa website mo. I know it was something I promised, and I'm sorry for taking so long to fulfill it. I hope it still serves you well, and I hope it becomes one less thing you have to worry about.

I promise you, this will be my last time disturbing your peace. I won't come back until I've become a better person. And I don't mean someone who has simply learned how to say the right words. I mean someone who has genuinely changed, someone who has learned how to face his problems, understand his emotions, and take responsibility for his actions.

Hindi ko man alam kung may bago ka na ngayon, kung may kausap ka na, or kung magkakaroon ka pa lang, I still wanna hold on to the promises I gave you, even if you no longer know about them. I don't know what the future holds, and I don't wanna make you feel like you have to wait for me. I only know that what I felt for you was genuine, and the love I had for you will always be a part of my life.

I'll still treasure you, kahit hindi ka na sa akin.

Please don't hate me forever for the things I've done. I know I don't have the right to ask you for forgiveness, and I know I don't deserve to ask you to remember me kindly. But I hope someday, when the pain has settled, you'll be able to remember me as someone who loved you, even if he failed to love you properly.

I'll take accountability for my actions. I'll accept the consequences of the choices I made. And I'll carry the lessons you've left me with, not to keep myself trapped in the past, but to make sure I don't become the same person again.

Mag-ingat ka lagi, especially sa pagmomotor. Please take care of yourself. I hope you always get home safely. I hope you never stop chasing your dreams. I know kaya mong abutin lahat ng 'yon. You're a good, strong, and capable woman, and I know there's so much more waiting for you in life. Don't stop growing. Don't stop becoming the person you've always wanted to be. Keep shining, even in places where I no longer get to see you.

And if life ever brings us to the same place again someday, I hope we'll meet as better versions of ourselves. I hope by then, we won't have to carry the same pain, the same anger, or the same regrets. I hope we'll be able to look at each other and understand how much we've grown.

I don't know if we'll ever meet again. I don't know if we'll ever have the chance to talk like we used to. I don't know if you'll ever feel the same way about me again. And as much as I wish I knew, I think I have to accept that some things are no longer mine to know.

For now, I'll respect your peace, your space, and the life you're building without me.

I loved you. I still do, in a way that I no longer wanna turn into anger or resentment. And maybe the hardest part of loving you now is learning how to let you go without pretending that you never meant everything to me.

You were someone I wanted to grow old with. Someone I wanted to marry. Someone I wanted to build a home with. Someone I wanted to come home to after a long, exhausting day. Someone I wanted to see beside me when life finally became easier. And it hurts knowing that those dreams might remain dreams.

But even if I no longer get to live those dreams with you, I hope you still get to live a beautiful life.

I'm sorry I wasn't the man you needed me to be when you needed me the most. I'm sorry I understood your worth when I was already losing you. I'm sorry for all the things I should've done, all the words I should've said, and all the love I should've given while I still had the chance.

Thank you for being my greatest love. Thank you for being someone I'll always be grateful to have met. And thank you for being a part of the person I'm slowly trying to become.

I hope life treats you gently. I hope you find the happiness, peace, and love you always deserved.

I don't expect a reply. You don't have to say anything. I only hope these words reach you with the sincerity they were written with.

Until we meet again, next time, my greatest love. <3
