A note from me to you

A few things I want you to know

Take your time with these. They’re written with all my love.

Thank you

For being my safe place

I just wanna say thank you, palangga, for all your love and support. Every time I’m down, lagi mo na i-snap away ang lungkot at pagod ko. You are my inspiration sa pang-araw-araw ko; every time I see you and talk to you, you brighten up my sad and tiring day.

Simula pa nung una, you’ve always been here by my side. You make me feel special—you showed the batang Kloyd na hindi siya mahirap mahalin. You helped me grow as a person and as your partner, and I’m so grateful to have a girl like you in my life.

Our environment and our time may change, but your love and support are still here. Words are never enough to say thank you and I love you, so I’ll give you my all. Hapiii first anniversary again, my hapiihapii. I love you always. ❤️

I’m sorry

And I’ll keep growing

I know I say this a lot, love, and I’m sorry for every disappointment, action, and behavior that hurt you. I didn’t notice at first; I thought it was okay because I could make up for it. But I realize now that I repeated mistakes and made you feel unloved.

I truly regret hurting you, especially when all you did was love and care for me. I didn’t know how to say sorry for all I’ve done, but I want you to know that I hear you. I want to make you feel that you’re not hard to love and that you’re worth choosing every day.

I’ll keep working to be a better man, my love. Please remember that you are loved by me.

Always

Here’s to our next chapters

I’ll keep pushing forward, my love, so we can make our dream come true. I want our future to be secure and happy; malayo pa pero malayo na, and it’s still the two of us in this story.

Thank you so much for existing in my life, my pretty palangga. Hapiii anniversary, my love. I love youuuuu always. ❤️
