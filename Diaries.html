The Damage I Can't Undo.

I hate the person I've been. Ang sinungaling ko, ang selfish ko. Sometimes, I feel like I deserve everything bad coming my way. Sana gantihan na lang din ako ng mundo. I'm so tired of myself. Sobra na. I don't even know what to say or what to do anymore. I'm mentally and emotionally drained, and the worst part is knowing na ako rin naman yung dahilan kung bakit ako nahihirapan ngayon. I want to become better. I want to learn from my mistakes and genuinely change. Pero tangina, I know I've caused too much pain already. Even Val got tired of hearing me talk about the same things over and over again. Nasaktan ko siya. To the point na kung hindi niya na lang ako mahal, matagal na niya siguro akong hiniwalayan because even she reached a point where everything became too much. And that's what hurts. Sobra na pala talaga. I don't even know how to make things right anymore because I know some wounds don't disappear overnight. I keep thinking about how the things I've done will stay with her, how she'll remember the moments when I made her feel that way. I can't help but feel guilty knowing na someone I love has to carry the pain I caused. Sa totoo lang, swerte pa nga ako kasi hindi niya pa ako binibitawan despite everything. Despite how exhausting I've been, despite all the times I've disappointed her. And here I am, still struggling with the same version of myself I've been promising to change. Ang bonak mo talaga, Kloyd. I wish Daddy were still here. I wish I could sit beside him and tell him everything. I wish I could tell him about all the things I've done wrong and ask him what I'm supposed to do. Gusto ko lang umiyak sa harap niya and admit how disappointed I am in myself. I wish he were here to tell me where I went wrong, to guide me, and to remind me that I still have a chance to become someone better. I'm so frustrated with myself. I don't think I can forgive myself for everything I've done. Sumabay pa talaga yung bigat ng regrets ko sa New Year. HAHAHA. Everyone is supposed to be welcoming a new beginning, pero here I am, carrying all these things I wish I could take back. Tatahimik na naman ang bahay, and I'll be left alone with my thoughts, remembering everything I've done wrong. Tangina, wala na bang bago sa sarili ko? Puro na lang disappointment. I keep asking myself how I'm supposed to fix everything when I've already hurt someone so much. Sometimes, I feel like disappointment is the only thing I've ever been good at. I don't know how to make up for everything, and I hate knowing na hindi sapat ang pagsasabi ko ng sorry para mawala yung sakit na naidulot ko. I regret how I treated her. She never deserved any of it. I hate knowing na naparamdam ko pa sa kanya na hindi siya enough, na hindi siya maganda, or that there was something lacking in her. Tangina, it was never true. Never. I never wanted her to feel that way about herself. She was never the problem, and she never had to question her worth because of me. But despite knowing all of that, I still made her feel otherwise. And that's the part I can't stop thinking about. How could I make someone I love feel so unloved? How could I let my own words and actions make her question something she should never have doubted in the first place? The guilt is eating me alive. I wish I could take back every word, every action, and every moment when I made her feel less than what she truly is. I wish I could go back and treat her with the gentleness and reassurance she deserved. I wish I had been more careful with her feelings. I don't know how I'll make it up to her. I don't know how to prove that none of those things I made her feel were true. I know I can't expect her to forget everything just because I regret doing it. I know she'll need time, and I know I have to take responsibility for the pain I've caused. I just wish I knew how to make her feel loved again. How to make her see herself the way I should have always made her feel. How to show her that she was never lacking, never ugly, and never someone who wasn't enough. I regret everything. And I mean everything. I'm sorry, Val. You never deserved to feel that way. I'm sorry I made you question yourself when I should have been the person reminding you how much you're worth. I don't want my apologies to become another set of empty promises. I want to become someone whose actions finally reflect how sorry he is. I'm so sorry.

10/23/24

Was I Enough?

I'm feeling it again. Idk. I don't even know where to start or how to explain what I'm feeling right now. I know naman na sometimes, wala akong kwentang kausap, especially when it comes to problems. Maybe I don't always know what to say, or maybe I don't know how to make things better. But it still hurts knowing na sa ibang tao niya kayang mag-open up, na sa iba niya kayang ikwento yung mga problema niya, while pagdating sakin, there's always silence. She would tell me na she'll talk about it later, na ikkwento niya rin sakin eventually. But when that time comes, she'll suddenly say, "I don't want to talk about it, please." And yet, I find out na sa iba naman niya naikukwento. I don't know. Maybe there's something about me that makes her feel like she can't open up. Maybe I really am the problem. But God, it hurts to feel like I'm someone she's supposed to be comfortable with, yet somehow, I'm not someone she chooses when things get heavy. Sometimes, I wish I had never opened her account and started reading those conversations. Maybe I would've been better off not knowing. But I did, and I know it's my fault too. I guess some things hurt more when you find them out yourself. Maybe I got too sweet. Maybe I became too understanding, too patient, and too willing to wait for things to change. Or perhaps I've grown so used to being the person who understands, adjusts, and waits that I no longer know what it's like to be understood without having to ask for it. Sometimes, I wonder if my only role in this relationship is to keep loving her, keep understanding her, and keep waiting, even when I don't know where I stand anymore. I want to find something to talk about. I want to make a conversation out of nothing just so we can talk again, just so I can feel that connection we used to have. But I guess even that feels different now. Maybe the silence itself is already telling me something. Maybe she doesn't want those conversations with me anymore. And I understand naman why. Kung ako rin siguro, minsan hindi ko rin kayang pakinggan yung sarili ko. I don't always say the right things, especially when it comes to her. Sometimes, my words and behavior only make things worse. And I know that because we've had arguments about it before. I know there were moments when even my behavior became exhausting for her. That's why I've been trying so hard to change. I keep looking back at the childish Kloyd I used to be, trying to become someone mature enough to meet her expectations. I wanted to be better for her. I wanted to become someone she wouldn't have to get tired of explaining things to. Someone who knows when to listen, when to understand, and when to give her space. But sometimes, I wonder if all those changes are still enough. Or if I've been trying so hard to become someone better that I've slowly forgotten how to be myself. It hurts whenever I think about how things used to be. I miss those days. I miss how things felt before. And whenever those memories come back, I can't help but feel sad. Sadly, we almost got into another argument because of it. Apparently, I miss my old life whenever I'm sad. And I guess she doesn't understand why I keep looking back at those moments. So now, I've been trying to change how I deal with my emotions. Whenever I feel sad or whenever I miss the old days, I don't express it as much anymore. I don't want to make it another problem. Napakaiyakin ko kasing OA dati, BAHHAHAH. But now, I'm trying to teach myself how to **be sad without crying**, how to be upset without letting it consume me, and how to keep things to myself without making anyone feel responsible for what I'm feeling. But sometimes, I wonder if I'm doing all of this because I've genuinely matured, or because I've become too afraid of showing how I feel. There was one time when I started thinking, what if habang tumatagal, paunti nang paunti yung care and affection niya sakin? What if the effort she used to give me is slowly fading? I know naman na hindi ibig sabihin nun na nababawasan na yung love niya. I keep telling myself that. But sometimes, her actions make me question things her words try to reassure me about. Maybe the love is still there, but the effort and eagerness aren't the same anymore. Maybe it's the little things that have slowly disappeared. And I can't help but notice them. That's where all the overthinking starts. And the sad part is, I'm even too scared to ask for reassurance. BAHAHAH. I don't know why, but I feel like asking for it would only make me look needy or make her feel like I'm demanding something she no longer has the energy to give. So instead, I keep everything to myself. I try to understand, I try to make excuses, and I try to convince myself that everything is fine, even when deep down, something feels different. Idk. It just hurts sometimes knowing na parang ang sama-sama ko na sa paningin niya. And that's fine, I completely understand naman. I know there are valid reasons behind how she feels about me. I know I have my own shortcomings, and I'm not going to pretend na wala akong pagkakamali. It's just sad. It's sad knowing that someone you love might see you differently now, especially when you still look at them with the same love you had before. I miss the old us. I miss how in love you were with me. I miss how you used to look at me, how you used to treat me, and how you made me feel like I was someone worth loving. I miss the little efforts, the excitement, the warmth, and the way you used to make me feel like I was your person. I miss everything about you. I even miss the way you used to see me as your dream man. Or was that only my dream? Sometimes, I wonder if I became too much. If my emotions, my overthinking, and all my OA tendencies made everything harder than it needed to be. Maybe that's why I keep questioning whether my feelings are even valid anymore. Maybe I've gotten so used to being told that I'm too emotional that I've started believing I have no right to feel anything in the first place. But I still feel everything. I still get hurt. I still miss things. I still want to be loved in the same way I used to be. And honestly, I'm starting to feel ashamed of myself. I keep thinking na wala naman talaga akong ibubuga. That I'm nothing special. That I'm a joke sometimes. Para akong babae sa relasyon na 'to, always emotional, always overthinking, always wanting reassurance. And I hate that I see myself this way. I hate feeling like I have to apologize for being someone who feels too much. I don't know. Maybe I'm still trying to figure out how to love someone without losing myself in the process. I want to be better. I want to be someone she feels safe with. I want to understand her without making everything about my own feelings. But sometimes, I wish someone would try to understand me, too. Because even the person who keeps trying to understand can get tired of pretending that nothing hurts.

11/24/26

Almost Real

I woke up today feeling empty and sad. I found myself zoning out again kasi napanaginipan ko na naman siya. Again. And somehow, this dream felt different. It felt so real, so familiar, and so painfully comforting all at once. We were playing Roblox when suddenly, she said, "Pst, Chat mo na ako ulit." I was caught off guard kasi parang totoong-totoo. I even woke up for a moment just to check my phone, hoping na baka totoo nga. Pero wala. It wasn't real. Panaginip lang pala. I went back to sleep, and somehow, the dream continued. She talked to me again. She told me na miss niya na raw ako and asked why we couldn't just talk to each other again. So I told her, "Sige, sige. Chat kita." And then I woke up. I just sat there, zoning out, thinking, shit, napanaginipan ko na naman siya. Ang saya namin sa panaginip na yun. We were playing, joking around, and laughing together. For a moment, it felt like nothing had changed. Parang bumalik lang kami sa dati. I could see her smile again. I could hear her laugh, even the little sounds she used to make when she laughed so hard. It's been such a long time since I last experienced those little things, and somehow, my mind brought them all back. Pero pagmulat ko, wala na siya. And God, it hurts. It's strange how my dreams keep bringing her back to me. How I get to see her, hear her voice, and feel her love again, even if only while I'm asleep. Then I wake up and have to remind myself that none of it happened. That she isn't here anymore. That I was only dreaming about someone I still miss so much. I don't even know if I can go through my day normally after experiencing something like this. It feels so heavy. It's like I got to have her again for a few moments, only to lose her all over again. I long for her. I miss her so much. At some point, I even told myself, sana hindi na lang ako nagising. Kasi kahit panaginip lang, I was happy there. I got to talk to her again. I got to hear her voice. I got to laugh with her. For a little while, I didn't have to miss her because it felt like she was still mine. I wanted to cry. Kasi the moment I opened my eyes, everything was gone again. Sometimes, I find myself wanting to sleep longer, hoping I could go back to those dreams. Kasi doon, she's still there. She still talks to me. She still laughs with me. And most of all, she still loves me. Sobra-sobra. The dream felt so gentle. She was so soft with me, so warm, so full of love. She made me feel like she missed me just as much as I missed her. And for those few moments, I got to experience everything I've been longing for. I wish it were true. I wish it wasn't just a dream. I wish I could wake up and find her still there. I wish we could still have those little moments together. I wish I still had her. But I don't. And maybe that's what hurts the most. I keep dreaming of her, and every time I do, I get a little piece of what I lost back. Only to wake up and realize that I still have to live without her. I dreamed of her again today. And for a moment, I was happy again. I just wish I didn't have to wake up to remember that she's gone from my life. I miss her so much.
