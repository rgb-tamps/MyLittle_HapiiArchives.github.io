11/24/26

I woke up today feeling empty and sad. I found myself zoning out again kasi napanaginipan ko na naman siya. Again. And somehow, this dream felt different. It felt so real, so familiar, and so painfully comforting all at once. We were playing Roblox when suddenly, she said, "Chat mo na ako ulit." I was caught off guard kasi parang totoong-totoo. I even woke up for a moment just to check my phone, hoping na baka totoo nga. Pero wala. It wasn't real. Panaginip lang pala. I went back to sleep, and somehow, the dream continued. She talked to me again. She told me na miss niya na raw ako and asked why we couldn't just talk to each other again. So I told her, "Sige, sige. Chat kita." And then I woke up. I just sat there, zoning out, thinking, shit, napanaginipan ko na naman siya. Ang saya namin sa panaginip na yun. We were playing, joking around, and laughing together. For a moment, it felt like nothing had changed. Parang bumalik lang kami sa dati. I could see her smile again. I could hear her laugh, even the little sounds she used to make when she laughed so hard. It's been such a long time since I last experienced those little things, and somehow, my mind brought them all back. Pero pagmulat ko, wala na siya. And God, it hurts. It's strange how my dreams keep bringing her back to me. How I get to see her, hear her voice, and feel her love again, even if only while I'm asleep. Then I wake up and have to remind myself that none of it happened. That she isn't here anymore. That I was only dreaming about someone I still miss so much. I don't even know if I can go through my day normally after experiencing something like this. It feels so heavy. It's like I got to have her again for a few moments, only to lose her all over again. I long for her. I miss her so much. At some point, I even told myself, sana hindi na lang ako nagising. Kasi kahit panaginip lang, I was happy there. I got to talk to her again. I got to hear her voice. I got to laugh with her. For a little while, I didn't have to miss her because it felt like she was still mine. I wanted to cry. Kasi the moment I opened my eyes, everything was gone again. Sometimes, I find myself wanting to sleep longer, hoping I could go back to those dreams. Kasi doon, she's still there. She still talks to me. She still laughs with me. And most of all, she still loves me. Sobra-sobra. The dream felt so gentle. She was so soft with me, so warm, so full of love. She made me feel like she missed me just as much as I missed her. And for those few moments, I got to experience everything I've been longing for. I wish it were true. I wish it wasn't just a dream. I wish I could wake up and find her still there. I wish we could still have those little moments together. I wish I still had her. But I don't. And maybe that's what hurts the most. I keep dreaming of her, and every time I do, I get a little piece of what I lost back. Only to wake up and realize that I still have to live without her. I dreamed of her again today. And for a moment, I was happy again. I just wish I didn't have to wake up to remember that she's gone from my life. I miss her so much.
